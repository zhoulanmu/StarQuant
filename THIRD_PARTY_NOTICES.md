# Third-Party Notices and Release Gate

This repository uses Qt 6 (Core, Widgets and Network). Before external distribution, the release owner must choose one of the following and retain evidence in the release record:

1. A Qt commercial license covering every applicable developer and the intended distribution; or
2. A reviewed open-source compliance package, including the exact Qt license texts, required notices, source/relinking obligations and any third-party notices for the delivered binaries.

The repository may also deliver MinGW runtime libraries, OpenSSL/TLS components, icons and other third-party dependencies. Generate an SBOM from the final package and review every component's license, copyright notice and redistribution condition before release.

The development endpoints for EastMoney, Sina, Tencent and news providers are not evidence of commercial display, caching, calculation or redistribution rights. A commercial release requires a signed data agreement and a source-attribution policy.
